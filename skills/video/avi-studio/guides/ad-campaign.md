# AD CAMPAIGN — the guide (seed)

*A seed, not yet built from a tutorial. It holds what we already know so Avi has one place to start; the
next ad or commercial tutorial the owner sends gets researched and folded in here (SKILL.md, "Updating a guide").
Where this seed is silent, use the matching step of `film.md`.*

Same studio loop as every guide: say the step → studio job → keep talking → the card lands with 2–4 options
→ the owner picks or fixes → lock → next.

## Steps

1. **The brief — interview, don't write.** Product, audience, the one thing the ad must make them feel or do,
   where it runs (Meta feed 4:5, Stories/Reels 9:16, YouTube 16:9, TV), length, offer, deadline, budget.
   Ask; don't assume. 🔒 the one-paragraph brief.
2. **Three concepts as stills.** One card, three options: a key frame and a one-line idea each
   (`nano-banana` or `gpt-image`, n 3). The owner picks one; numbered fixes. 🔒 the concept.
3. **Product as an element.** The product photographed or rebuilt as a clean sheet on grey (front, 3/4, back)
   — `higgsfield-product-photoshoot` or `gpt-image` edits from the owner's photos. The product must not drift:
   "Product consistency yes, character consistency no" for Marketing Studio [l7W3QzU8w5s] — a human lead goes
   through `film.md` §2 (sheet, one face) instead.
4. **Storyboard + all keyframes as stills first.** "Generate all keyframe stills first, get approval, THEN
   animate. Stills cost ~$0.04–0.10 each; animations cost ~$2.42 each. A bad still caught before animation
   saves the animation" (avi-video-pipeline). 🔒 the board.
5. **Motion.** Takes per `film.md` §7 (house prompt, draft first, four takes, numbered fixes). Branded
   avatars / UGC-style presenters: Higgsfield Marketing Studio via the `avi` engine (`higgsfield-generate`).
6. **Cut-downs and formats.** One master edit, then each placement's aspect and length; captions burned in for
   sound-off feeds. One folder card per format.
7. **Ship.** Scheduling to Meta is an outward action: The owner's explicit go, every time.
