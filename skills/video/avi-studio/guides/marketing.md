# MARKETING — the guide (seed)

*A seed, not yet built from a tutorial. It routes the marketing skills we already have through one loop; the
next marketing tutorial the owner sends gets researched and folded in here. For anything with a story or a
recurring human character, use `film.md`; for one product push, `ad-campaign.md`.*

Same studio loop: say the step → studio job → keep talking → card with 2–4 options → the owner picks or fixes →
lock → next.

## Steps

1. **What and for whom.** The business (each of the owner's businesses), the channel (Instagram, TikTok, the website,
   a listing), the goal (leads, trust, a launch), how much content, by when. 🔒 one paragraph.
2. **The look.** A brand board as one card: palette, type, three reference frames (`nano-banana`, n 3–4).
   A dealership-style website rule still holds: verified data only, manufacturer sources, hide what isn't verified.
3. **Pick the tool by format** (each is a skill the `avi` engine can drive):
   - UGC / street interview / unboxing / review / ASMR sets → `higgsfield-content-factory` (5 formats, a
     content plan first, then batches);
   - product stills, lifestyle, hero banners, carousels → `higgsfield-product-photoshoot`;
   - marketplace listing cards → `higgsfield-marketplace-cards`;
   - a real room or vehicle edited → `gpt-image` edits (higgsfield-generate).
4. **Plan before generating.** The content plan (formats × count) is its own card; the owner approves the plan
   before credits are spent. Show the price in the sentence that starts a batch.
5. **Batches as cards.** One format at a time, 4 options per card, pick → fix → lock. Keep the winners in the
   project folder under one name each.
6. **Publishing is the owner's.** Posting, scheduling and anything public is an outward action: his explicit go.
