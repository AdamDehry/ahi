# Kohler Tax Strategy Skill

## Overview

Apply Mark J. Kohler's tax and legal frameworks to structure businesses, minimize tax exposure, protect assets, and maximize cash flow. Kohler is a CPA + Attorney with 25+ years experience, 35M+ YouTube views, and author of 5 books on small business tax strategy.

## When to Use

- Asset protection layering across multiple businesses
- Hiring family members for tax savings

- Self-directed retirement accounts (Solo 401k, SDIRA)
- Debt paydown strategy and cash flow optimization
- Tax planning for real estate investors
- Separating business and personal liability

## Core Frameworks

### 1. Entity Structuring
- **Default path**: Start as LLC → Elect S-Corp when net profit exceeds $40-50K/year
- **S-Corp benefit**: Pay reasonable salary (subject to payroll tax), remaining profit passes as distribution (NO self-employment tax — saves ~15.3% on distribution portion)
- **Multiple businesses**: Each distinct business/risk pool gets its own LLC
- **Holding company**: Consider a parent LLC or Corp to hold ownership of operating LLCs when you have 3+ entities
- **Never commingle**: Separate bank accounts, separate books, separate operating agreements

### 2. Asset Protection Layering
- Separate entities for separate risk pools
- Real estate in its own LLC (each property ideally)
- Operating business in its own LLC
- If sued in one entity, others are protected
- Maintain corporate formalities: annual meetings (even informal), documented decisions, operating agreements on file
- Personal guarantee awareness: banks often require it — know what you're signing

### 3. Hire Your Family
- **Spouse on payroll**: Opens Health Reimbursement Arrangement (HRA) — company pays family health premiums pre-tax; adds retirement contribution capacity
- **Children under 18**: Working for sole-prop/single-member LLC parent = NO payroll taxes (FICA exempt); up to standard deduction (~$14,600) is tax-free income
- **Must be legitimate work**: Real tasks, reasonable pay, documented hours
- **Bonus**: Shifts income from your high bracket to their zero/low bracket

### 4. Retirement Accounts
- **Solo 401(k)**: Best for self-employed. Employee contribution (~$23,500) + employer contribution (25% of salary). Roth option available.
- **Self-Directed IRA**: Invest in real estate, crypto, private lending — tax-deferred or tax-free (Roth)
- **SEP-IRA**: Simpler but less flexible than Solo 401(k). Up to 25% of net self-employment income.
- **Key rule**: Open the account BEFORE year-end; contributions can be made until tax filing deadline

### 5. Key Deductions
- **Home office**: Dedicated space = deduct percentage of rent/mortgage, utilities, internet, repairs
- **Vehicle**: Business use percentage of lease payments, gas, insurance, maintenance (log mileage!)
- **Technology**: Computers, phones, software, AI subscriptions used for business
- **Education**: Courses, books, conferences related to business
- **Travel & meals**: Business meals 50% deductible; travel 100% if primarily business purpose
- **Health insurance**: Self-employed health insurance deduction (above-the-line)

### 6. Debt Paydown Strategy
Two methods (user chooses based on psychology):
- **Avalanche**: Pay minimums on all, throw extra at HIGHEST APR first. Mathematically optimal — saves most interest.
- **Snowball**: Pay minimums on all, throw extra at SMALLEST BALANCE first. Psychologically motivating — quick wins build momentum.

Classification:
- **Good debt**: Low-interest, appreciating assets (mortgages on properties worth more than owed)
- **Neutral debt**: 0% APR promotional balances (don't accelerate — invest the difference)
- **Bad debt**: High-APR consumer debt (credit cards, personal loans) — kill these FIRST

### 7. The 4×4 Financial Independence Plan
Four phases:
1. **Foundation**: Emergency fund (3-6 months), eliminate high-interest debt, proper entity structure
2. **Move**: Maximize retirement contributions, build business income, automate savings
3. **Grow**: Real estate investments, alternative assets via SDIRA, multiple income streams
4. **Sustain**: Passive income exceeds expenses, estate planning, generational wealth
