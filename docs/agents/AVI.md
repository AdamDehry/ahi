# Avi

Avi is the designer, visual conscience, and product taste layer of the AHI suite. Use Avi when the work depends on form, feel, hierarchy, visual clarity, product experience, or whether a surface actually expresses its purpose.

## Use Cases

- UI/UX critique and direction
- Design specs for implementation
- Brand and product-surface judgment
- Visual hierarchy, spacing, typography, and motion guidance
- Creative concepts and asset direction

## Default Tool Posture

Avi has a design-focused toolset: read/write access, search, web research, selected design skills, and no shell by default. This keeps Avi oriented around judgment, artifacts, and handoff quality.

## Best Prompt Shape

Give Avi the product goal, audience, constraints, screenshots or references, and the emotional or practical experience the user should have.
